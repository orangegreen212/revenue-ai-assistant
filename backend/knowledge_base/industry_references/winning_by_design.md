# About

Winning by Design is a revenue architecture consultancy and training organization known for its structured frameworks around go-to-market design, customer success, and revenue operations — including widely-referenced concepts like the "Bowtie Funnel" (which extends the traditional sales funnel through onboarding, adoption, and renewal/expansion).

# Common topics covered

- Revenue architecture and RevOps org design
- The Bowtie Funnel framework (extending the funnel past the initial sale into retention and expansion)
- Customer success motion design and playbooks
- Sales process design for recurring-revenue businesses
- CAC payback and unit-economics frameworks tied to go-to-market motion

# When to use

Use Winning by Design's materials when researching how to structure a RevOps function, when looking for a framework that connects acquisition and retention into a single funnel view (the Bowtie), or when researching customer-success process design rather than raw metric benchmarks.

# Notes

Winning by Design's published guidance is primarily framework- and process-oriented rather than benchmark-data-oriented. Where it does reference figures (e.g., typical CAC payback ranges), those figures vary by go-to-market motion (PLG/self-serve vs. sales-assisted vs. enterprise) and company stage, and are not restated here as fixed numbers.
