# About

The Bessemer Cloud Index is a public market index and accompanying research published by Bessemer Venture Partners, tracking the performance and operating metrics of publicly-traded cloud and SaaS companies. Bessemer also publishes the "State of the Cloud" report, which is frequently cited for its framework-level thinking on SaaS metrics (e.g., the "Rule of 40," the "T2D3" growth framework).

# Common topics covered

- Public cloud company valuation multiples relative to growth and retention
- Net Revenue Retention benchmarks among top-performing public SaaS companies
- The Rule of 40 framework (revenue growth rate + profit margin)
- Efficiency metrics (Magic Number, CAC Payback) at scale
- Growth trajectory frameworks for early-to-late-stage SaaS companies

# When to use

Use Bessemer's materials when researching how retention and efficiency metrics correlate with public-market valuation, or when looking for widely-referenced strategic frameworks (like Rule of 40) rather than raw benchmark tables.

# Notes

Benchmark and framework values from Bessemer vary depending on:

- Whether the company being compared is public or private (public-company benchmarks are not directly transferable to early-stage private companies)
- The specific year's Cloud Index composition and market conditions
- Company growth stage

This document intentionally does not restate specific numeric benchmarks, since Bessemer's public figures are tied to specific report years and the composition of public cloud companies changes over time. For current figures, consult Bessemer's latest published Cloud Index or State of the Cloud report directly.
