# About

OpenView is a venture capital firm that publishes annual SaaS benchmark reports based on surveys of hundreds of SaaS companies. These reports are widely cited across the SaaS operating and investing community as a reference point for how key metrics trend across company stage and ARR band.

# Common topics covered

- Net Revenue Retention (NRR) and Gross Revenue Retention (GRR)
- CAC Payback Period
- Growth Efficiency / Burn Multiple
- Sales & Marketing spend as a percentage of revenue
- Expansion revenue and upsell motion benchmarks
- Rule of 40 (growth rate + profit margin)

# When to use

Use OpenView's reports when you want to compare a specific company's metrics against a broad cross-section of SaaS companies at a similar ARR stage, or when researching how "typical" ranges for a metric shift as a company scales from early-stage to growth-stage.

# Notes

Benchmark values in OpenView's reports vary depending on:

- Company stage and ARR band (e.g., <$1M, $1–10M, $10M+)
- Customer segment (SMB vs. mid-market vs. enterprise)
- Sales motion (self-serve/PLG vs. sales-assisted vs. enterprise sales)
- The specific report year — benchmarks shift over time as market conditions change

This document intentionally does not restate specific numeric benchmarks from any particular report year, since those figures change annually and citing an outdated number as current would be misleading. For current figures, consult OpenView's latest published report directly.
