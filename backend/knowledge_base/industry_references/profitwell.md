# About

ProfitWell (now part of Paddle) publishes free SaaS metrics research, tooling, and educational content focused heavily on subscription pricing, churn, and retention. Its resources are commonly cited for practical, operator-focused explanations of metric definitions and retention tactics.

# Common topics covered

- Churn reduction tactics (voluntary vs. involuntary churn, failed-payment recovery)
- Pricing and packaging strategy for subscription businesses
- MRR movement and reporting best practices
- Customer retention benchmarking methodology
- Free tooling/calculators for common SaaS metrics

# When to use

Use ProfitWell's resources when researching practical churn-reduction tactics, pricing strategy, or when you need an operator-friendly (rather than purely academic) explanation of a SaaS metric.

# Notes

Benchmark figures published by ProfitWell vary depending on:

- Company size and stage
- B2B vs. B2C subscription model
- Pricing model (per-seat, usage-based, flat-fee)
- The specific year of the underlying research

This document intentionally does not restate specific numeric benchmarks, since ProfitWell's published figures are tied to specific studies and time periods. For current figures, consult ProfitWell's latest published research directly.
