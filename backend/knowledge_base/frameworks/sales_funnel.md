# Definition

The sales funnel describes the staged path a prospect follows from first awareness of a product to becoming a paying customer — typically: Visitor → Lead → Marketing Qualified Lead (MQL) → Sales Qualified Lead (SQL) → Opportunity → Closed-Won. Funnel analytics track the volume and conversion rate at each stage.

# Why it matters

The funnel shows exactly where revenue growth is leaking. A strong top-of-funnel with a weak SQL-to-Close conversion points to a sales execution or ICP-fit problem; a weak top-of-funnel with a strong win rate points to a demand-generation problem. Without stage-by-stage visibility, teams often optimize the wrong lever.

# Formula

Stage conversion rate:

Conversion Rate (A → B) = Count reaching Stage B / Count entering Stage A

Pipeline coverage (a common sales-planning check):

Pipeline Coverage = Total Pipeline Value / Revenue Target

Simplified sales velocity:

Sales Velocity ≈ (Opportunities × Win Rate × Average Deal Size) / Sales Cycle Length

# Example

This month: 2,000 leads → 500 MQLs (25% lead-to-MQL) → 150 SQLs (30% MQL-to-SQL) → 60 opportunities (40% SQL-to-opportunity) → 18 closed-won deals (30% win rate).

Overall lead-to-customer conversion = 18 / 2,000 = 0.9%

# Common mistakes

- Comparing conversion rates across teams or time periods when stage definitions in the CRM aren't consistent.
- Chasing top-of-funnel volume that never turns into real pipeline (vanity metrics).
- Ignoring the time lag between stages — this month's MQLs often become next quarter's revenue, not this month's.
- Blending self-serve/PLG signups and enterprise sales-assisted leads into a single funnel when they behave completely differently.
- Tracking stage counts without also tracking win rate, deal size, and cycle length — the funnel shape alone doesn't explain revenue outcomes.

# Related metrics

- **Pirate Metrics (AARRR)** — the funnel's product-led-growth counterpart, extending the same logic into activation and retention.
- **CAC** — funnel efficiency is a major driver of acquisition cost.
- **Customer Segmentation** — funnel conversion often varies significantly by segment and should be reviewed accordingly.
